01 / Camera chain

One camera, five links.

Open the rig explorer →

A house camera leaves the body on SDI, crosses to the rack on SMPTE hybrid fiber when a camera-end converter is fitted, and lands on a switcher input. The return runs back the same way and carries the viewfinder picture, tally and camera control.

Return 1 carries five services

One failed leg, five symptoms

On the rack converter, Return 1 carries program, camera control, tally, genlock and intercom. If that leg fails, the viewfinder picture and shading control go together, which matches Camera 1 on September 11.

At FMP, genlock is not in use and fiber talkback is deliberately unused. Comms run on Clear-Com, which the audio department manages.

Known issues
  • Camera 1, September 11: the front SDI output stopped feeding the viewfinder and CCU control was lost; the other bodies were fine. Tracked as two faults, the front SDI output and the control/return behavior. Return 2 restored a usable viewfinder feed. Do not merge them until a controlled test proves a common cause.
  • Camera 3, reported September 17: a curved, color-fringed arc split the frame warm and cool. Cause: the ND wheel was not fully seated. Reseated in the field and it now clicks firmly into each detent. If it drifts again without anyone touching it, flag the detent for service.
  • Camera 2: the right side handle clamp is broken and runs on a magic arm. Do not try to tighten it.

Settings that catch people

  • Camera ID: the switcher Camera ID set on the body must equal its switcher input. A wrong number makes a live camera look dead on the camera control panel.
  • Per-output monitoring: the LCD, front SDI and main SDI each have their own MONITOR page. Clean feed, status text, zebra and focus assist set on one output do not carry to another. Set operator overlays on the front SDI page only, never the main SDI.
  • Reference: referencing to the switcher return needs program selected as the reference source in Setup.

Power

4-pin XLR 12–18 V DC from the supplied 100 W adapter, a V-Lock battery, or fiber-delivered power through the camera-end converter. External power takes priority and fails over to the battery. Which method is live at each position is not recorded.

Two lens types by sight

The September 1 frames show a second, visibly different optic with a white and green focus scale in feet and meters and an iris scale of 1.9, 2.8, 4, 5.6, 8, 11 and 16. That corroborates two distinct lens types before either model number is read.

Focus through the zoom

When a lens and body are paired differently, or a shot is sharp at telephoto but soft at wide, run the back-focus procedure for the lens actually on the body. Reading a model number does not supply the procedure.

04 / Camera fiber converter

The camera end of the fiber.

Converter specifications ↗

Blackmagic pairs one camera-end converter with one rack converter per camera. Three rack units are live, so three camera-end units should exist. One has been seen. Look at the back of the body you are building before you trust the fiber.

What has been seen

September 14, 2026
  • One body with a camera-mounted box feeding three short BNC jumpers into SDI OUT, SDI IN and REF IN/TC IN, plus a large round multipin connector consistent with a male SMPTE 304. A camera-end converter is fitted on that body. Its model is inferred; the plate is unread.
  • A separate frame shows a body whose SDI OUT runs to a single coax with no converter jumpers. It cannot tell a second body on copper from the same body seen from another side.
  • Neither capture recorded the camera position.

If a body has no converter, SDI is its live path. That matters most at front of house, where SDI needs reclocking beyond roughly 200 ft.

Mounts on the rear of the body by bracket. The battery plate comes off and the V-Lock plate rides on top of the converter. 19.1 × 14 × 13.8 cm, 2.2 kg.
IN THE BOX
Converter, mounting bracket and three short 12G-SDI jumpers. No camera-end spare exists if all three are fitted.

FMP rules
  • Do not connect a headset to the converter's 5-pin XLR. Fiber talkback is unused at FMP; Clear-Com is the comms path.
  • Do not connect or disconnect hybrid fiber until the responsible technician confirms fiber power is off.
  • Cap the SMPTE connector the moment the fiber comes off. Two cap sets exist unfitted, and the Head of Video will not reuse a cable that took dust.

Power

Powered over the SMPTE hybrid fiber from the rack unit, which also powers the camera and its accessories through the Molex. If fiber power drops, the camera on its own 4-pin XLR, or a V-Lock battery on top of the converter, can carry it. Firmware: unplug the fiber, power the camera from mains, then use Blackmagic Camera Setup over USB-C.

05 / Rack fiber converter

The rack end of the fiber.

Blackmagic fiber converters ↗

Three Blackmagic Studio Fiber Converters are racked and live, one per body, with no spare. A fourth unit is the only way to cover a failure without dropping a camera to SDI.

Identity

Hardware verified · September 1, 2026
QUANTITY
3 racked and live · CINEURSANWFRSTUD · labels and serials not read
FORM
2RU half-rack width. The front carries the SMPTE connector, USB-C, a 5-inch LCD and the power switch, so the unit mounts facing the rack rear by design. 21 × 8.8 × 37.2 cm, 2.8 kg.
POWER
Single IEC inlet, 100–240 V AC. It sends about 200 V over the fiber to the camera end, with a power handshake and a 30 mA imbalance cutoff. It carries the camera and camera-end load, so budget the circuit accordingly.
CONFIGURATION
All on the front LCD. PTZ source is SDI (switcher) or 9-pin; talkback source is SDI plus DB-25, or DB-25 only. The language prompt returns after every firmware update.
Failures on record
  • September 1: one unit dropped on a firmware fault and was recovered by reflashing through the front USB-C with Blackmagic Camera Setup. The unit and version were not recorded.
  • September 11: Camera 1 lost its return (viewfinder and CCU control). Check that unit's LCD first.

Connections

FRONT
SMPTE 304 hybrid fiber (female) · USB-C
REAR INPUTS
3 × 12G-SDI return in · REF IN · DE-9 PTZ (RS-422) · DB-25 intercom/tally · IEC
REAR OUTPUTS
2 × 12G-SDI camera out · REF OUT · 4 × XLR analog audio out
NOT THE FIBER PORT
The blue optical I/O cage on the rear is a 10 GbE port the product does not activate. It is not the camera fiber and not a spare network port, so an idle cage proves nothing about fiber service.

September 14 · rack position not identified

Front-of-house loop

When a tour asks for a house front-of-house camera iso and accepts a loop, the 12G-SDI camera outputs here are the probable loop point, not anything at the camera. Settle it before the advance.

Camera build & strike →

Each check below is a look, a photo or a read, not a repair. Record what you find with the date and position so the next person does not repeat it.

Bodies

  • Read the serial numbers on all three bodies, with clearance from the Head of Video.
  • Confirm each body's switcher Camera ID matches its input, Camera 1 first.
  • Record how each position is powered: fiber, battery or mains.

Lenses

  • Photograph the plates on the second 16× and the 22×.
  • Tag each barrel to the position it was on.
  • Trace what drives the FMM-X1 module, and from where.

Camera-end fiber

  • Look at the rear of the other two bodies and count converters.
  • Record whether SMPTE caps are fitted at breakdown.
  • Confirm the return jumper from the converter to Camera 1's SDI in.

Rack-end fiber

  • Read and record each unit's firmware version from its LCD.
  • Label each unit with its camera number.
  • Confirm which output feeds the switcher and which is free for a front-of-house loop.
  • Confirm what Returns 2 and 3 carry.
  • Photograph each front panel with the SMPTE 304 connector mated.
