Explore the model

Choose any part from the Component menu or tap its shape. Views change the angle; Common parts select and locate a component.

Touch: tap a part. Turn on touch rotation in Views to drag or pinch the model.

Mouse: drag to orbit; Shift + scroll to zoom. Keyboard on the model: arrows rotate, +/− zoom, Home fits the equipment. Escape turns touch rotation off. All parts are available in the Component menu.

Scroll inside the reading panel for Use, Check and photo references. Expand reading for more space; Show model brings the model back. Tab between controls, or use the panel tabs and arrow keys to change sections.
