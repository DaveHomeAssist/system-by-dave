    Reference views & scope

    What is grounded

    Front, rear and side placement follows the four supplied product views. Connector types and hardware counts were checked against Blackmagic Design’s HD8 ISO specifications. FMP labels come from the supplied draft Switcher SOP.

    Panel geometry, button travel and lever motion are approximate. Product-photo illumination is not the venue’s current state. No installed firmware, current venue patch or physical equipment readiness is verified.

    Practice boundary

    Preview, direct Program selection, CUT, AUTO, a manual background MIX, BKGD and video FTB are simulated. Keyers, audio, macros, Aux routing, recording, streaming and shifted banks are described for identification only.

    The FMP SOP is a draft. Its source labels are useful context; uncertain fallback and downstream-routing statements are not modeled as confirmed facts.

    Full reference photographs are included in the downloadable guide.
