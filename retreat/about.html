About New Bethlehem

New Bethlehem is a Catholic retreat for women who are grieving or experiencing regret after an abortion.

The retreat takes place over one day. It was written with women in mind whose work or family responsibilities make it difficult to spend a weekend away.

Sharon Delaney

Sharon Delaney is a freelance writer who homeschooled her sons. Her inspirational stories have appeared in Guideposts, Reader’s Digest, and Writer’s Digest.

For the National Catholic Register, Sharon has told the story of a father and son finding faith in recovery and of a Coptic Catholic parish bringing a shuttered New Jersey church back to life. In her own family’s story, she recalls praying to St. Joseph for a cabin in the woods.
