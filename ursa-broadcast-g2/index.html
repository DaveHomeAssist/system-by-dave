Field reference

Six faces, three control panels.

The G2 ships with a B4 mount and includes an EF mount; PL and F mounts are optional. The 12 pin broadcast lens connector powers and controls servo B4 and PL glass. The left side carries three panels: the forward panel on the chassis, the ergonomic panel on the outside of the fold-out monitor, and the internal panel behind it.

The forward panel is the show panel.

Everything an operator touches with the monitor closed lives here. Gain is in dB on this body, shutter is a speed, and ND is a physical wheel.

Field reference

The status LCD answers most questions without opening the monitor.

It shows external and generated timecode, the main timecode or clip duration, FPS, shutter angle, white balance, gain, iris, battery in 25 percent bars (hidden on mains), card 1 and 2 with an SD flag, a record dot, a LOW warning near five minutes remaining, a dropped frame flash and peak audio meters.

For a shaded camera on a live switch the usual set is Iris A, Zoom S, Focus M. With B4 glass set the record sensor area to 4K; 5.3K and 6K are Super 35 areas for EF, PL and F lenses, and Blackmagic RAW is fixed at Ultra HD from the B4 area.

Field reference

Check back focus on every lens swap.

  1. Place a high contrast chart as far away as practical, about seventy feet.
  2. Open the iris fully and hold exposure with the ND wheel so depth of field stays shallow.
  3. Zoom fully in and focus on the chart.
  4. Zoom fully out. Sharp means done.
  5. If soft, identify the exact lens and its back-focus method before adjustment. Use the electronic calibration procedure for an electronic lens, or the flange focal length ring only on a mechanical lens. Follow the linked method guide; stop if the lens method is unclear.
  6. Zoom in and out slowly and repeat until focus holds through the whole range.

Field reference

Set the camera ID and reference before the first bars check.

  1. Menu, Setup: set ATEM Camera ID to the switcher input number.
  2. Setup: Reference Source to Program unless every camera and the switcher share a house sync, in which case External everywhere. Change it during setup only; the outputs drop briefly when the camera re-times.
  3. Match the record format to the switcher standard or the return and camera control will not lock.
  4. Switcher program or aux to 12G-SDI In, camera 12G-SDI Out to the switcher input. A fiber converter carries both on one SMPTE cable.
  5. Monitor settings: grids on for the LCD; the red tally border only draws with grids on.
  6. Setup: Color Bars on to confirm the path at the switcher, then off.
  7. Handgrip iris to A, then lock the forward panel.

Shader panel changes to gain, shutter, white balance, iris, lift, gamma, gain RGB and master black arrive over the SDI return and update the camera readouts. Zoom stays on the rocker with iris on A.

Open checks stay open until observed.

  • Record the installed camera software before trusting any menu description here.
  • Confirm the return path reaches this body: red border on the LCD when the switcher takes it.
  • Confirm the handgrip switch positions before handing the camera to the shader.
