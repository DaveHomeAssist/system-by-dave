    How to play

    1. The center word is planted for you. Select a tile or clue and type your answer. Tap a tile again, or press Space, to switch between Across and Down.
    2. Correct answers bloom automatically. Answers of eight or more letters grow vines that can reveal one letter in a nearby clue. Solve two crossing answers with a shared theme to earn a golden wildcard seed.
    3. Select a seed in the soil and plant it to reveal one letter in a nearby unsolved clue. The arrows slide it across soil until an edge or obstacle stops it. A move must leave a clue within reach.

    Use the date arrows to visit other gardens, or tap the date to preview and choose a puzzle. Finish a garden to unlock Replay, which saves separately from your original solve. Progress stays in this browser.
