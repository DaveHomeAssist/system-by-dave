EVIDENCE BEFORE HYPE

What makes a shop hot?

Heat combines rating quality and recent public discussion. Confidence tells you how much evidence supports that score. Neither score establishes food quality, safety, or citywide popularity.

Heat = 95% × available weighted components + 5% × source diversity
ComponentHow it works
Rating quality · 55% weightEach 0–5 rating is shrunk toward a 3.8 prior using 50 prior reviews. Providers are combined using log(1 + review count). If review timestamps are available, their weight decays with a 90-day half-life.
Social buzz · 40% weight60% decayed mention volume, 25% engagement quality, 15% sentiment. The window is 365 days, with a 90-day half-life. Weekly mentions are displayed separately.
Diversity · 5% weightEach additional independent scoring source adds one third of the diversity score, capped at 100.
Confidence · separate35% source count, 35% freshness, 30% sample size. Below 60 is labeled low confidence. It does not increase heat.

When a component is unavailable, its weight is removed and the remaining component weights are normalized. The panel explains the gap. With no eligible ratings or mentions, heat is unknown. Scores are not comparable to a provider’s star rating.

Which sources are supported?

  • OpenStreetMap: explicit cheesesteak cuisine tags and coordinates. Discovery can miss shops with incomplete tagging. Map data is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL.
  • Licensed feed and editorial JSON: business data, ratings, and verified public mentions. Operators must have permission to store, display, and derive scores from each field. Every record includes attribution and expiry.
  • YouTube Data API: up to 50 recent search results per refresh, followed by video metadata and statistics. Matched mentions are display-only, excluded from heat, sentiment, and confidence. They expire after 24 hours unless refreshed.
  • Google, Yelp, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and X: no default scraping or enabled adapter. Each requires an approved integration and applicable licenses. A Google key alone does not authorize caching Google ratings on this OpenStreetMap map.

Discovery and active status

Coordinates must fall inside Philadelphia’s published city boundary. Neighborhood labels can be resolved with Robert Cheetham’s neighborhood polygons. Records merge only when source IDs match, or strong name/address or name/phone evidence agrees within 100 meters. Branch locations stay separate.

Two independent recent active sources establish confirmed active. One recent active source yields likely active. Conflicting, missing, or older-than-30-day status evidence yields uncertain. Two recent permanent-closure sources establish permanently closed. A recent temporary closure yields temporarily closed unless evidence conflicts. Permanent, temporary, and uncertain locations are excluded by default; explicitly include them with the status filter.

Matching discussion without guessing

Automatic matching requires an unambiguous full shop name of at least two words. Posts mentioning several shops stay in an editorial queue. Verified editorial records can identify a shop explicitly. False negatives are preferable to assigning a viral post to the wrong business.

Duplicate text and canonical URLs are suppressed for scoring. Obvious affiliate, giveaway, promotional, and link-spam patterns are excluded. This heuristic cannot detect all manipulation. Engagement uses comments and saves with higher weight than likes; where views are available, interactions are normalized by views. Missing engagement remains explicitly unknown in the source record.

The sentiment classifier uses a short positive/negative vocabulary and a two-word negation window. It cannot reliably understand sarcasm, dialect, or complex mixed opinions. YouTube content bypasses this classification.

Freshness, gaps, and removal

Daily scheduled ingestion is recommended. Each provider has its own success/failure history. Failed sources preserve only unexpired prior data. The interface warns when a source fails or the most recent successful refresh is more than 48 hours old. Raw archives expire within seven days, YouTube records after one day, and other records within 30 days or their earlier license expiry.

Use “Report incorrect information” in any shop panel for corrections or removal requests. Reports enter an editorial queue; they do not change rankings automatically. An operator must process the queue. We make no claim of complete Philadelphia coverage.
