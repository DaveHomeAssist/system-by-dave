PHILLY CHEESESTEAK HEATMAP

A little less guesswork.
A little more wit.

This map helps you explore Philadelphia cheesesteak shops, compare available ratings, and understand recent public attention. It keeps evidence and uncertainty visible.

Demo means demo

With no live configuration, all shops, addresses, ratings, reviews, mentions, and hours are fictional fixtures. Coordinates demonstrate the Philadelphia map. These are not real business recommendations. The demo clock is fixed at September 28, 2026.

Privacy

Theme and saved shops stay in your browser’s local storage. Geolocation requires your permission and is used only for local distance filtering. It is not stored or sent to the application server. OpenStreetMap’s tile service receives normal browser requests, which reveal your IP address and viewed map area.

Correction reports do not request a name, email address, or account. Do not include private information. Live reports are retained for up to 90 days; demo reports are not saved. When a trusted reverse proxy is configured, an HMAC of the request IP is used for a one-minute rate-limit bucket; raw IP addresses are not stored by this application. Hosting providers may maintain their own infrastructure logs.

Data corrections and removal

Open a shop and select “Report incorrect information.” Choose “Data removal request” to flag data for removal. Operators review these reports; requests are not automatically approved. Public API data is kept only within configured retention periods.

Coverage and attribution

OpenStreetMap contributors provide the basemap and optional discovery records under ODbL. Philadelphia supplies the city boundary. Neighborhoods are attributed to Robert Cheetham under CC BY 4.0; neighborhood boundaries are approximate and can be disputed.

No paid placement influences the score. A high score is a description of the available data, not an endorsement or a guarantee that a business is currently open.
