PHILLY CHEESESTEAK HEATMAP

A little less guesswork.
A little more wit.

Find a cheesesteak spot, see what people have been talking about, and check the sources behind the heat. Public attention is a starting point for exploring Philly, not a verdict on who makes the best steak.

Real shops, saved snapshot

This edition includes 28 real Philadelphia shops in a snapshot dated October 1, 2026. Shop locations and available business details come from OpenStreetMap. Public Reddit posts from the year leading up to the snapshot supply the discussion signal.

This is a saved view, not a continuously updating feed. Heat reflects the available evidence at the snapshot date; a quiet shop may simply have little discussion in the collected sources. Coverage is incomplete, and shop-name matches can be wrong. Check a shop’s own website or call before heading over.

This snapshot has no star ratings or review counts. Those values are unknown, not zero.

Search-volume data is not available in this snapshot, so it does not contribute to heat. The current scores use the available public discussion signal.

Privacy

Theme and saved shops stay in your browser’s local storage. Geolocation requires your permission and is used only for local distance filtering. It is not stored or sent to the application server. OpenStreetMap’s tile service receives normal browser requests, which reveal your IP address and viewed map area.

Data corrections and removal

This public edition is read-only and does not accept correction forms. To flag an incorrect listing, source match, or removal request, open an issue in the project repository. GitHub requires an account, and issues are public: include the shop and source link, but no private information. Reports need review and do not change the map automatically.

Coverage and attribution

OpenStreetMap contributors provide the basemap and optional discovery records under ODbL. Philadelphia supplies the city boundary. Neighborhoods are attributed to Robert Cheetham under CC BY 4.0; neighborhood boundaries are approximate and can be disputed.

No paid placement influences the score. A high score is a description of the available data, not an endorsement or a guarantee that a business is currently open.
