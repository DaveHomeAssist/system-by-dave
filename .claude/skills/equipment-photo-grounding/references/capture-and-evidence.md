# Capture and evidence reference

## Choose views by the claim

| Claim to establish | Useful evidence | Insufficient by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact model/revision | Readable model plate plus an overall view linking the plate to the unit | Similar enclosure or a generated render |
| Control identity and location | Close-up retaining adjacent labels and an orientation landmark | Isolated unlabeled knob |
| Selector's present state | Readable legend and pointer in the same original view | Manual diagram of another setting |
| Connector function/direction | Exact port label matched to model-specific documentation | Connector shape or cable color |
| Attachment relationship | Side/underside view showing the clamp, mounting joint, and both parts | Overlapping silhouettes |
| Cable endpoint/installed route | Both endpoints or a dated trace record, scoped to the rig | One plugged-in cable |
| Hinge or swivel movement | Multiple positions, direct observation, or mechanical documentation | One pose or an animated approximation |

There is no universal minimum number of photos. Ask for the view that distinguishes the remaining possibilities. Keep serial numbers and unrelated people out of public output unless needed and authorized. Follow venue photography permissions.

## Record claims, not one confidence score per image

Use the project's existing evidence vocabulary. If absent, use:

- **Confirmed:** supported by direct evidence for the stated claim, scope, and date. This can confirm a photographed label without confirming present operation.
- **Documented:** stated in a named manual or maintained record, without field verification of the installed state.
- **Reported:** attributed to a person, with date/context if known.
- **Inferred:** reasoned from stated evidence, with the inference explained.
- **Unknown:** missing sufficient evidence.
- **Contradicted:** credible sources disagree; keep both locators and the resolution needed.

Separate sources from claims. Assign `source_id`; record type, locator, capture/retrieval date when known, revision, and original/derived relationship. For a derived image keep its parent source and edit description. Do not manufacture capture dates or numerical confidence percentages.

## Resolve disagreement by scope

A manufacturer's drawing can establish the normal function while a recent photograph establishes a different installed accessory. A recent record is not automatically more accurate than an earlier direct observation. A user's correction governs the requested representation but does not prove a hidden wiring path. Preserve both claims until the discrepancy is resolved.

## Case-derived cautions

The FMP discussion contained corrections from a visible LCD to the wrongly labeled hidden converter, from a supposed viewfinder to a handle, and from an integrated focus grip to an added clamp-on control. Use these as examples of identification failures, not as current inventory evidence. The discussion's original photographs were not all re-inspected when this skill was written.
