Before the show

  1. Use the agreed house startup procedure and approved show files on both Studios.
  2. Check all four projection surfaces and the deck strips against the agreed walk-in/opening look.
  3. Coordinate one agreed cue and reset with lighting and A1. A repeated cue may also replay sound.
  4. Check fresh projector readings and report readiness to stage management.

House check Startup order, file paths, warmup time, opening look, safe test/reset cue and who gives the ready report.

During the show

Follow the called show and agreed cue list. Keep Projctl Show Lock on for performances. It covers Projctl commands; Millumin has its own command path.

If an image or cue is wrong, tell stage management and the assigned house contact. Give the lane/surface, time and what changed. Treat missing or stale readings as unknown.

House check Who watches the status, which sound cues share triggers, and the agreed response to warnings.

If something goes wrong

Coordinate with stage management before repeating a cue. Check with A1 so audio is not replayed unintentionally.

Use only the agreed fallback. Get the video lead’s authorization before changing inputs, mapping, display settings or Show Lock, or restarting equipment.

House check The missed-cue fallback, restart/recovery steps and who can authorize them. Walk through these steps with the house team.

At the end of the call

Coordinate shutdown with stage management and the duty manager. Follow the agreed on-site sequence, allow the required projector cooldown, and record unresolved faults.

House check Exact shutdown order, cooldown, overnight/between-show power states and the completion check. Use the agreed house shutdown sequence.

Who handles what

  • Lighting runs the called visual cues.
  • A1 coordinates linked sound and agreed manual audio cues.
  • Stage management authorizes show interruptions.
  • Dave is the setup and technical-handoff contact.
