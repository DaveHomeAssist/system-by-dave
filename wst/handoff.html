Before the show

  1. Use the house startup procedure and show file 3.1 on the M5 Studio, and the deck pixel show on the M1 Studio.
  2. Check all four projection surfaces and the deck strips against the walk in and opening look.
  3. Coordinate one agreed cue and reset with lighting and A1. A repeated cue may also replay sound.
  4. Check fresh projector readings and report readiness to stage management.

During the show

Follow the called show and agreed cue list. Keep Projctl Show Lock on for performances. It covers Projctl commands; Millumin has its own command path.

If an image or cue is wrong, tell stage management and Dave. Give the lane or surface, the time and what changed. Treat missing or stale readings as unknown.

If something goes wrong

Coordinate with stage management before repeating a cue. Check with A1 so audio is not replayed by accident.

Use only the agreed fallback in the crew checklist. Check with Dave before changing inputs, mapping, display settings or Show Lock, or restarting equipment.

At the end of the call

Coordinate shutdown with stage management and the duty manager. Follow the house shutdown sequence, allow the projector cooldown, and note any fault for Dave.

Who handles what

  • Lighting (Katy) runs the called visual cues.
  • A1 (Ryan) coordinates linked sound and manual audio cues.
  • Stage management authorizes show interruptions.
  • Dave supports video remotely by text or email until strike.
