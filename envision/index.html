    AfterJinx · a lineup reveal with billing still open

    AfterJinx lineup drop. More tentacles around the edges. I’m still waiting on the final lineup.

    Image prompt

    Create a 4:5 artwork plate for an underground bass afterparty flyer.
Frame the edges with curled deep-sea tentacles with hot orange
highlights against black and inky teal. Keep the top center clear
for a supplied event title. Leave a wide dark zone below it for one
headliner mark and a calm middle area for rows of support marks.
Keep the bottom low-detail for the event information. Artwork only,
without lettering or logos.

    AfterBreak · keep the layout, change the temperature

    Make this icier. Same layout and mystery headliner. Need a story too.

    Edit prompt

    Shift the background accents to ice blue and cool chrome. Keep
the black base, existing title, question mark, artist marks,
billing order, and footer in place. Apply the new color to the
background linework and edge texture. Keep contrast behind the
artist marks and text.

    Story adjustment
    Rebuild at 9:16. Keep the mystery mark near the top, stack support marks in the middle, and pull the date and CTA inward so interface overlays leave them visible. Use the same approved copy and supplied files.

    Justin Hawkes · use a supplied portrait

    Use the Justin Hawkes flyer for layout. I have an approved artist photo. Make this one warm and smoky.

    Image prompt

    Create a 4:5 background for a portrait-led drum and bass flyer.
Reserve a centered chest-up silhouette area for the supplied cutout.
Wrap smoke, curved metallic shapes, and warm amber rim light around
the portrait zone. Fade the lower third to dark charcoal for the
headliner and support billing. Keep an open band near the bottom
for event details. Artwork only, without faces, lettering, or logos.

    Placement
    Add the supplied photo at center. Put the headliner directly below the portrait, followed by the supplied support marks and the event details.

    An FAQ story

    Carry over one small campaign motif. Build a dark, low-detail text area for a question and a short answer. Give dates, hours, and location their own slides when that clarifies the message.

    AfterJinx FAQ story copy

    Format notes

    • 4:5 feed keeps the title, focal image, and first billing tier visible at thumbnail size. Use a clearly defined footer.
    • 9:16 story gives the layout more height. Reflow the billing or split a dense announcement across slides. Keep key text clear of top and bottom interface overlays.
    • 4 × 6 in print is a 2:3 trim ratio. Confirm the printer's bleed and safe area, extend edge artwork through the bleed, and keep type and any real QR code inside the safe area.
    • FAQ story uses editable type over a simple plate. Keep one answer per slide and verify midnight date wording against current event copy.
