## Email signup (AV by Dave demos)

The demos on avbydave.com (`/demos/`) have an optional "Want the next AV problem to play with?" form after each demo is completed.
It's the only place this site asks for personal information.

- **What's collected:** the email address you type. If you arrived from a link
  with campaign tags (for example from Instagram), the tags in that link
  (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) may be stored with
  your signup so I can tell which post brought you. Nothing else is added.
- **Who handles it:** the address goes straight from your browser to
  [PROVIDER NAME] ([link to the provider's privacy policy]), which stores the
  list and sends the emails. This site never stores it.
- **What it's used for:** emailing you when a new demo or related resource from
  AV by Dave is ready. About [N] emails a month at most. It's never sold or
  shared for anyone else's marketing.
- **Confirmation:** you'll get one email asking you to confirm. If you don't
  confirm, you won't get anything else.
- **Leaving:** every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also email
  avbydave@gmail.com and ask to be removed or for a copy of what's held. I'll
  act on it within [30] days.
- **How long it's kept:** until you unsubscribe or ask for removal, then
  [deleted / kept only as a suppression entry so you aren't re-added], subject to
  [PROVIDER NAME]'s own retention.
- **Legal basis (UK/EU visitors):** your consent, given by submitting the form
  and confirming. You can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.
- **Spam protection:** the form has a hidden field that people never see. It
  isn't stored or sent.

The demo works fully without signing up.
