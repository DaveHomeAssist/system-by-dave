Relay connecting

Never

  • Never cut power at a breaker, a power strip or a mains switch. A lamp projector must cool with its fans running, about 3 minutes.
  • Never shut down, log out of or unplug the show computer or its control monitor. Pending decision
  • Never open or save the show file.
  • Never touch lens, focus or zoom.

If something is wrong

  • A unit will not turn on: wait 3 minutes, try once more, then call the show backup.
  • A warning light, a cover error or a lamp message: photograph the screen and the indicator lights, then call the show backup or the video lead.
  • A or D say "Not reached by the relay": normal while the playback app is open. Go by the light on the surface.
  • The relay is offline: this page can send nothing. Call the video lead.

What this page does

  • Lanes B and C are powered from this page. Their words come from the relay's own reading.
  • Lanes A and D are powered with the handheld remote. Their ticks and timers are yours, not readings.
  • A tick lapses after 30 minutes. Look again and tick again.
  • After a projector lights, leave it alone for 60 seconds.
