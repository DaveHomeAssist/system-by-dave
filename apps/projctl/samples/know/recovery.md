# Sample recovery steps

A sample for the simulator, not a real procedure. The real steps live in the private documents
folder on the relay computer.

## A surface is wrong: least disruptive first

1. Shutter. Any time, mid-show too. A lit lane with its shutter CLOSED is dark on stage: open it from Control.
2. Input. Before a show. Read the lane's input on Control. If it is flagged, tell the video lead. Do not touch the playback app.
3. Power cycle that one projector. Before a show. Respect cooling: a lamp unit needs about 170 s to cool, then 75 s more before it can light again. Never cut mains while it cools.
4. Relaunch the playback app. Before a show, all four on, and only after any approved change is saved. Quit without saving, reopen the approved show file, wait for four fullscreen outputs.
5. Restart the show computer. Only on the video lead's word.

The cooling and relight waits, and that a power cycle leaves the show computer's outputs
alone, are researched, not yet tested on the rig.

## A cue does nothing

1. Open the MIDI test tab and read "last cue received".
2. The cue is listed with the right time: it arrived. Look at the playback app.
3. The cue is not listed: it never arrived. Check the console, the cable and the interface.

## The page says CHECK BY EYE

The relay has no current reply from that projector. Nothing on the card is a reading until it
comes back. Look at the surface.
